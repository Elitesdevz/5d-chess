The contributor commissioning this integration reports permission to reuse and
adapt Overrun for 5d-chess. This records the source and the reported permission.
Original third-party credits and rights remain with their respective creators.

Overrun's assets/bots/ely.glb and assets/bots/trooper.glb provide the two
multiplayer player skins. Overrun credits the character rigs and shared idle,
walk and run clips in assets/bots/anim/ to Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com/).
The bundled city versions in public/overrun contain in-place rotation clips
sampled from those FBXs and same-origin WebP versions of the original textures.
The shipping glTF meshes and animation tracks were optimized with gltfpack.
Seating, resting, city jobs and figure placement were adapted for 5d-chess.

The five weapon meshes and first-person gloves in public/overrun were baked
from the pistol, smg, ar, shotgun and sniper builders in src/gunmodels.js.
The city groups meshes by material, simplifies their geometry and shares each
model between the shop rack, first-person view and multiplayer characters.
The catalog WebP product images are renders of these same five meshes.
Overrun inspired the sprint, aim, firing feedback and magazine/reload controls.
City movement limits, prices, hit points, timings and custody rules are local.
