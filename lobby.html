How Elo works

One Elo rating covers Live multiplayer at every player count. Classic and AI duels are unrated. Every account starts at 1200.

Duels use standard Elo with K = 32. In multiplayer, 85% of the rating weight follows finishing order and 15% follows elimination performance. If nobody earns an elimination, finishing order receives the full weight. Stronger opponents offer bigger gains.

Multiplayer changes are normalized across opponents, balanced to total zero and settled once. Simultaneous eliminations tie; shared king captures split elimination credit. Completed Live multiplayer games award Elo and coins. Classic awards coins without Elo; AI rewards are based on daily victories. Local games award neither.
