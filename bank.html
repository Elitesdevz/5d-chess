How loans work

Borrow up to half of your wallet balance, subject to the reserve and the 12,000 coin limit. Interest is added at midnight in the Netherlands on unpaid principal. Pay early or in parts. Overdue loans accrue 0.9% per day, and total interest is capped at 25% of the original loan.

Payments cover interest first, then reduce principal.
